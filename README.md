# MVA Robotics study guide

Concise, unofficial study notes and practice questions for the MVA Robotics course. Learn the concepts through short external explanations, then check your understanding with questions and answer checkpoints. The scope follows the course; the learning route does not require reading its slides first.

1. [Introduction to robotics](guides/0-introduction.md)
2. [Kinematics and inverse kinematics](guides/1-kinematics.md)

4. [Motion planning](guides/3.motion_planning.md)
5. [Optimization and control](guides/4-optimization_control.md)
6. [Perception and estimation](guides/5-estimation.md)
7. [Reinforcement learning for robotics](guides/6-reinforcement_learning.md)

Each guide gives a learning route, key ideas, questions and answer checkpoints. Start with the selected pages of the unofficial **MVA Robotics 2026 Notes**. Explain the ideas in your own words, use a targeted video when helpful, then answer 10–15 questions from the companion **Practice Quiz**. Use the guide’s open-ended questions and the official sample to check your understanding.

The notes and companion quiz are shared separately; see [references](references/README.md) for access. Each guide gives suggested PDF viewer pages, quiz question numbers and corrections. Both PDFs cover lectures 2–7, so also review the introduction guide. Some statements in these resources need qualification. They do not predict quiz questions.

The linked external explanations are particularly useful for [kinematics](guides/1-kinematics.md), [contact and friction](guides/2-simulation_dynamics.md), [planning](guides/3.motion_planning.md), [MPC](guides/4-optimization_control.md), [Kalman filtering](guides/5-estimation.md) and [RL](guides/6-reinforcement_learning.md). Use them to resolve gaps, not as a required playlist. Video durations were checked against publisher listings; descriptions, available transcripts and article text were inspected, but the videos were not independently watched. Reading times are estimates.

Course slides provide a coverage and terminology check, and associated exercise concepts are in scope. Slide page references use **PDF viewer page numbers** from the 2026 local versions. The external resources use different examples and do not cover every course-specific detail. The exercises and official sample remain important checks.
